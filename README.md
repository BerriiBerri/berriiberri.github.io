===<div id="text" style = "font-family: Sweetie Pie; color:#ffa6d8">About me</div>===
I am '''21 years old''' and I live in the US. After rediscovering Sonic through his TV shows, I stumbled across the Virtual Arcade port of ''Sonic the Hedgehog 2'' when it was new and decided to buy it. I had previously found ''Sonic Jump Fever'' on Google Play when it was still new as well (it has now been removed from the Play Store, unfortunately).

My dream was to become an animator, but then I decided I wanted to be a missionary/teacher. While I still want to do the latter, it's probably not going to work out as I intended (which is normal in this sort of thing), but my mom would much rather me not go to college to do that unless it's a place we can afford; which means really, really cheap. I'm currently trying to get a small business off the ground in order to fund my education, which will take some time. In the meantime, I'm working a part-time job to fund the business. I'd also like to get my student loan from my first year of college paid off so I'll have more funds to allocate to my education.

Spice the Cat is a Sonic fan character based off of the ''Sonic the Hedgehog'' games. She lives with her precious Chao, Sugar, in a small urban area outside Station Square. She dreams of one day becoming a good enough artist to open her own business doing commissions.

During the Eggman War, Spice enlisted as a member of the Resistance as a field agent, where she met her future best friends Jesma and Jasma the Rabbits (they're twins), Nicholas the Cat (Nick for short), Shroud the Cat, and Bluish the Chameleon. To this day, they vow to protect their home from minor threats in the event Sonic and/or his friends are not around to save everyone.

Born with the ability to manipulate gravity, Spice rarely uses this skill except on the battlefield. However, it is an incredibly useful maneuver, as Spice does not need to focus all her energy into using it. Once someone is trapped in one of her gravity-bending orbs, they will stay at that rate of gravitational pull forever, until she changes it back to normal.

When Spice is not with her friends, on a mission, or drawing, she is with her closest companion and Chao, Sugar. Having raised Sugar from an egg herself as a youngster, Spice and Sugar are inseparable and can communicate with each other, almost inaudibly, at times. Sugar can often sense when Spice feels upset about something, even if she has hidden her feelings from everyone else; however, due to Sugar's naïve nature as a Chao, this sometimes goes unnoticed even by her.

When Spice was still living with her family, she was often subjected to the narcissistic treatment of her mother, due to all the stress her mother was under as a single parent. For this reason, Spice developed a disorganized attachment style. As soon as she was able, she left her home to find her way in the world.

===<div id="text" style = "font-family: Sweetie Pie; color:#ffa6d8">Notable Adventures</div>===
*First destination was Empire City.
*Her first trip renewed her love for the city life, and she ended up finding her way to Station Square.
*Decided that Station Square was a bit too congested for her tastes, so she settled with a house nearby the city limits.
**However, during her time with the Resistance, she and the rest of her team built a fort that they still use for long-term missions.
*She ran into Amy Rose while doing some sight-seeing in Station Square. They hit it off rather well.

===<div id="text" style = "font-family: Sweetie Pie; color:#ffa6d8">Personality</div>===

Spice hates staying cooped up at home for too long, and as a result, she finds herself visiting locations all over the world. However, she enjoys being with her close friends, especially Nick the Cat, as he is the only non-Chao friend that she has ever bonded with so deeply. Because of her disorganized attachment, she often builds up walls between herself and others, while at the same time trying to maintain a bright, friendly personality. This causes her to feel tired after conversations occasionally, but she is very sensitive and sweet deep down.

She loves anything kawaii and secretly desires to convert to the kawaii lifestyle, so all of the clothes she wears are kawaii. Sugar is the only one who knows of this, and she enjoys helping Spice pick cute clothes. However, she also tries to convince Spice to embrace who she is and reveal her lifestyle choice to her friends. Unfortunately, Spice always chickens out and never does.

===<div id="text" style = "font-family: Sweetie Pie; color:#ffa6d8">Powers and abilities</div>===

Spice was born with the ability to manipulate gravity. With little-to-no concentration, she can target any object or entity and reverse or amplify its gravitational pull, either pulling the target upwards or pushing it downwards. This makes her a formidable gravity-bender, being more adept than others like her, given the mental power required to use this ability is quite significant. She is also endowed with super speed, capable of reaching speeds comparable to that of Sonic the Hedgehog.

Spice is also an excellent singer. She enjoys singing to her favorite songs as a hobby, and occasionally shares sound files of her singing with her friends. She is a contralto, but she can reach notes up to the soprano range as well. Spice also possesses cooking skills, making her the de-facto chef in her friend group while away on the battlefield. When not fighting, she loves to make her own Chao food for Sugar using Chao fruit and other all-natural ingredients. She also enjoys making treats, both for Sugar and herself (or any of her friends). Additionally, she can speak to Chao, often aiding her friends in conversations with Sugar. Lastly, Spice can also draw portraits quite well and wishes to one day sell them to the public.
